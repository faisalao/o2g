Privacy Policy: Calendar Busy Sync

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who uses it

Calendar Busy Sync is a personal tool. It is run by a single person, for their own calendars, on a computer they own. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

It requests one Google permission: calendar.app.created. This allows it to create its own secondary calendars and to add, change and remove events in those calendars only. It cannot read or change any other calendar, and it has no access to Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

How the data is used

The tool copies the owner's own meeting times (and, where the owner chooses, titles and meeting details) from their desktop calendar into those Google calendars, so that the owner's scheduling page shows when they are busy. It never invites or emails anyone.

Storage and sharing

  • All processing happens on the owner's own computer. There is no server and no third-party service.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on that computer.
  • No data is sold, shared, transferred to others, or used for advertising or analytics, and none of it is used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the calendars the tool created removes all data it wrote.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
