Calendar Busy Sync

A small personal tool that copies the owner's own meetings from a desktop calendar into Google Calendar, so that the owner's scheduling page knows when they are busy.

  • It is used by one person, for their own calendars, on their own computer.
  • It runs locally. There is no server, no account to create, and nothing to download here.
  • In Google, it can only see and change calendars that it created itself. It has no access to any other calendar, email, contacts or files.

This app is not offered to the public and does not accept new users.
